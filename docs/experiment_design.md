# A/B Experiment Design and Sample-Size Planning

## Purpose
Estimate the sample size needed to detect a difference between two independent
proportions, and compare recruitment requirements across target churn rates.

## Method
- Two independent groups with equal allocation.
- Two-sided statistical test.
- Significance level (alpha): 5%.
- Statistical power: 80%.
- Sample size estimated using a normal approximation.
- Recruitment targets adjusted for expected attrition.

## Illustrative Example
With a 10% baseline churn rate and an 8% target churn rate:
- Absolute reduction: 2 percentage points.
- Relative reduction: 20%.
- Usable sample size: 3,213 per group.
- Recruitment target with 10% attrition: 3,570 per group.
- Total recruitment target: 7,140.

## Scenario Analysis
The planner compares target churn rates of 9.5%, 9%, 8%, and 7%.
Smaller detectable improvements generally require larger samples, while
larger improvements can be detected with fewer participants under the
same statistical assumptions.

## Limitations
These are planning estimates, not observed product results. Actual
experiments may require adjustments for multiple comparisons, unequal
allocation, clustering, repeated testing, or other design considerations.
Attrition is an estimate, and actual retention may differ.
